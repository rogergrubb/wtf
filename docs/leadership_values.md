## CORE MISSION & THESIS

**Official Mission Statement:**
"We are building AI to simulate the world through merging art and science."

**Long-term Vision:**
Building foundational General World Models (GWM) capable of simulating all possible worlds and experiences.

---

## WHAT THEY EXPLICITLY VALUE: Key Beliefs

### 1. **World Models as the Frontier**
"We believe that world models are at the frontier of progress in artificial intelligence."

**Why it matters:**
- They position GWM as the most important architectural direction for AGI
- This signals their judges will weight solutions showing understanding of world dynamics
- Language model approaches alone are seen as insufficient

### 2. **The Trial-and-Error Learning Thesis**
"Language models alone won't solve the world's hardest problems – robotics, disease, scientific discovery. Real progress requires models that experience the world and learn from their mistakes, the same way that humans do. And this kind of trial and error can be massively accelerated when done in simulation, rather than in the real world."

**Keywords they repeat:**
- "simulation" (North Star concept)
- "experience the world"
- "trial and error"
- "learn from mistakes"

**Implication for hackathon:** Solutions showing learning/iteration capability, simulation mindset, or embodied reasoning will rank higher than static generation.

### 3. **Simulation as Path to General-Purpose AI**
"World models offer the most clear path to general-purpose simulation."

This is foundational to their research direction. They're not building specialized video generators—they're building simulation engines.

### 4. **The Next Frontier of Intelligence**
"The next frontier of intelligence will come from models that can understand, perceive, generate and act in the world."

**The sequence matters:**
- Understand → Perceive → Generate → **Act**

This tells you action/agency is central to their vision. Static video demos may underweight compared to demos showing an agent making decisions, adapting, or controlling something.

## RESEARCH PRIORITIES (Ranked by Emphasis)

### **Tier 1: General World Models (GWM-1) — THE NORTH STAR**

GWM-1 is positioned as their flagship research direction. They describe it as:
"State-of-the-art General World Model built to interact with the real world and simulate reality in real time. Interactive, controllable and general-purpose."

**Three implementations they highlight:**
1. **GWM Worlds** — Explorable environments. Open-ended interactive world simulation. "A new frontier for open-ended interactive world simulation. A way of building infinite explorable realities in real-time."
2. **GWM Avatars** — Conversational video agents. Real-time virtual personas.
3. **GWM Robotics** — Robotic manipulation. Simulating physical interactions and learning how actions unfold.

**Signal:** If your hackathon entry involves GWM-1 API or shows understanding of world dynamics (predicting how actions cascade), you're aligned with their core thesis.

### **Tier 2: Video Generation (Gen-4.5)**

"The world's best video model, featuring state-of-the-art motion quality, prompt adherence and visual fidelity. Offers unprecedented visual fidelity and creative control with cinematic and highly realistic outputs."

**What they emphasize here:**
- "Motion quality" (not just statics)
- "Prompt adherence" (control matters)
- "Creative control" (they value precision, not just speed)
- "Cinematic" (professional aesthetic standard)
- "Realistic" (grounded in reality, not fantasy-first)

### **Tier 3: Real-Time Video Agents (Runway Characters)**

"Fully autonomous and real-time virtual video agents capable of natural conversation and contextual awareness."

Built on GWM-1. Combines world models with characters. Key here: **contextual awareness and conversational agency**. They're not interested in just puppetry—these agents should understand context and respond intelligently.

**Notable:** Zero fine-tuning required to generate expressive digital personas from a single image.

### **Tier 4: Multimodal Simulation**

"Pioneering general-purpose multimodal simulators of the world."

This is their research frontier. The fact they call it "multimodal" (not just video) suggests they value solutions that integrate multiple input types: video, text, audio, spatial data, etc.

- **"Contextual awareness"** — Understanding context matters as much as raw generation.
- **"Creative control"** — Creators' intent should be precisely expressible and honored.

## WHAT THEY DON'T SEEM TO VALUE

Based on what's **absent** from their messaging:

- **Static imagery alone** — All emphasis is on video, dynamics, and real-time interaction.
- **Generic/gimmicky demos** — They emphasize "unprecedented visual fidelity" and "cinematic" quality, not novelty for novelty's sake.
- **Monolithic models** — They break things into components (worlds, avatars, robotics), suggesting modular, composable systems are valued.
- **Uncontrollable generation** — "Control" and "precise control" appear repeatedly. Unpredictable or hard-to-steer outputs would underweight.
- **Passive consumption** — Everything emphasizes agency, interaction, and action. Passive viewing experiences are not highlighted.

## SYNTHESIS: What Judges Are Looking For

Based on publicly stated priorities, Runway hackathon judges will likely weight entries on these dimensions:

### 🔴 **CRITICAL (North Star Alignment)**
- Does it involve **General World Models** or demonstrate **world modeling capability**?
- Does it show **simulation-based thinking** (understanding how dynamics cascade)?
- Does it involve **interactive/real-time** interaction rather than batch generation?

### 🟠 **HIGH (Core Values)**
- **Precision/Control**: Can creators direct the output precisely?
- **Agency/Action**: Does the system make decisions or adapt based on context?
- **Multimodal**: Does it integrate multiple input/output types?
- **Professional quality bar**: Is it cinematic/production-ready, not just novel?

### 🟡 **MEDIUM (Enablers)**
- **Robotics/Physical simulation**: Any embodied learning component?
- **Conversational/contextual awareness**: Does the system understand context?
- **Specific domain value**: Film, architecture, education, science?

### ⚪ **LOWER (Nice-to-Have)**
- Novel use of existing APIs
- Impressive motion quality alone
- Creative concept without technical depth

## ACTIONABLE TAKEAWAYS FOR YOUR HACKATHON ENTRY

1. **Lead with world models or simulation thinking.** If your project shows you understand how systems evolve and can predict/control dynamics, you're aligned with their core thesis.

2. **Emphasize real-time interactivity.** Batch processing is fine; real-time control is better.

3. **Show precise creative control.** Judges want to see creators directing the system, not fighting it.

4. **If using characters/avatars, emphasize agency and contextual awareness.** Stiff puppetry won't win; adaptive, smart agents will.

5. **Target a professional domain if possible.** Film, architecture, robotics, or science. They're building for institutions, not just casual users.

6. **Merge technical depth with aesthetic quality.** "Merging art and science" is their stated philosophy. A beautiful, creatively considered output beats a technically flashy one.

7. **Consider embodied/learning components if feasible.** Trial-and-error, iterative improvement, or physical simulation all map to their values.

8. **Use their language in your pitch.** Phrases like "world modeling," "interactive simulation," "creative control," and "real-time" will resonate with judges who've internalized the company's direction.
