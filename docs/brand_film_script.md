# WTF — Brand Film Shooting Script (v1 LOCKED)

**Working title:** *Number One Son*
**Internal codename:** *Whiskey Tango Foxtrot*
**Total runtime target:** 100–105 seconds
**Aspect ratio:** 16:9, 1920×1080, 30fps
**Audio:** stereo, 48kHz
**Submission target:** Runway API Hackathon, Mon May 11, 2026, 09:00 ET

---

## Cast

- **Cloned-Roger voice** — narrator throughout. Trained from Mastermind's 5–10 minute voice sample (Custom Voice Training endpoint).
- **Real Roger** — closing shot only, ~5 seconds, on-camera.
- **Roger's mother** — appears in two photographs (1969 at age 25, hospital ~75). Not voiced.
- **Roger's son** — appears in Golden Gate photo. Not voiced.
- **Roger's family** — appears across the photo set. Not voiced.

## Cast — Asset Origin

- **Live-action footage:** Roger's self-recorded closing shot.
- **Photographs:** seven uploaded real photos from Mastermind (`mastermind_real_*.jpg`), gently restored via Runway Super-Resolution + gentle gen4_image references where needed.
- **AI-generated visuals (Act 2):** Runway gen4_image, gen4.5, gen4_aleph (the recursion: tools used in the film are tools used to make the film).

## Final Script

### COLD OPEN — 0:00 – 0:10

**0:00 – 0:04 — BLACK FRAME**

*[White text fades in, centered, sans-serif, large.]*

> **My mother used to call me her Number One Son.**

**0:04 – 0:06 — BLACK FRAME, hold.** Beat. Silence.

**0:06 – 0:10 — Second line fades in below.**

> **And that gave me something to live up to.**

*[No narration in cold open. Letters do the work. White on black. Minimum 2 second hold on full text.]*

### ACT 1 — THE LIFETIME (10–34s)

**0:10 – 0:14 — DISSOLVE to AUG 69 PHOTO**

*[Beach photo. Mom at 25, young Roger ~5 in back, his sister ~3 in front. Restored gently from the original print — color cleaned but AUG 69 stamp preserved. Hold for 4 seconds.]*

**Narration begins (cloned-Roger voice, slow, steady):**

> *"So I lived."*

**0:14 – 0:16 — Cut to TEENAGE ROGER PHOTO** *(Norwegian-style sweater, late teens/early 20s).*

> *"I worked."*

**0:16 – 0:18 — Cut to AI-GENERATED CARPENTRY SHOT** *(Runway gen4_image: hands working wood, real tools, sawdust, soft golden light. Hero shot.)*

> *"I built furniture."*

**0:18 – 0:20 — Cut to AI-GENERATED CONSTRUCTION SHOT** *(Runway gen4.5: framing of a house, blue sky, real workmanship.)*

> *"I built homes."*

**0:20 – 0:22 — Cut to AI-GENERATED COMMERCIAL BUILDING SHOT** *(Runway gen4_image: completed commercial structure, professional setting.)*

> *"I built commercial buildings."*

**0:22 – 0:24 — Cut to PROFESSIONAL HEADSHOT** *(Real photo. Adult Roger in shirt and tie.)*

> *"I built businesses."*

**0:24 – 0:27 — Cut to GOLDEN GATE WITH SON** *(Real photo. Roger and his little boy.)*

> *"I built a family."*

**0:27 – 0:30 — Cut to TODDLER-FLYING-AT-COAST photo** *(Real photo. Roger holding the toddler's hands so she can pretend to fly. The "wings" image.)*

> *"And I taught them to fly."*

*[New line. Carries the toddler image. Was originally just "I built a family" but this richer two-beat earns the second photo.]*

**0:30 – 0:34 — Cut to HOSPITAL THREE-GEN PHOTO** *(Real photo. Mom at ~75 holding the newborn. Roger leaning in. Same woman from cold-open photo, fifty years later. Hold.)*

> *"And every time the world changed,"*

**0:34 – 0:36 — HOLD on hospital photo.**

> *"I asked myself the same question."*

**0:36 – 0:38 — HOLD continues. Slow camera push-in (Ken Burns) toward the mother's face in the hospital photo.**

> *"Am I still living up to it?"*

**0:38 – 0:40 — Beat. Hold on her face.**

**0:40 – 0:42 — Voice changes register. Quieter, almost confiding.**

> *"And then this happened."*

*[Hold on her face for one extra moment, then dissolve.]*

---

### ACT 2 — THE CRAFTSMAN'S PASSES (42–95s)

**0:42 – 0:46 — Dissolve to AI-GENERATED workshop establishing shot** *(Runway gen4.5 video: a craftsman's workbench, but futuristic, glowing tools, half-physical half-digital. ~3 second pan.)*

> *"The smartest AI we've ever experienced."*

**0:46 – 0:50 — Tools laid out. The toolbox UI from previous iterations is here, but as a craftsman's workbench, not a military arsenal. Each tool labeled.**

> *"And like every craftsman before me, I picked up the tools."*

**0:50 – 0:55 — FIRST PASS demonstration. A tool is drawn from the workbench (visual: floats up, glows). Cut to: a rough Runway gen4_image generation of a target shot. Recognizable but unrefined.**

> *"First pass — rough. The shape of an idea."*

**0:55 – 1:00 — SECOND PASS. Different tool drawn. Same shot, refined — Runway gen4.5 rendering with motion. Detail emerges.**

> *"Second pass — clarity."*

**1:00 – 1:05 — THIRD PASS. Aleph (gen4_aleph) tool drawn. The shot gets a cinematic color grade. Teal-and-orange filmlook.**

> *"Third pass — the cut."*

**1:05 – 1:10 — FOURTH PASS. ElevenLabs tool drawn (eleven_multilingual_v2 / dubbing). Audio waveform layers in. The shot has voice now.**

> *"Fourth pass — the voice."*

**1:10 – 1:15 — FIFTH PASS. Characters / gwm1_avatars tool drawn. A face appears in the shot — a narrator avatar speaks for one beat.**

> *"Fifth pass — the soul."*

**1:15 – 1:22 — Cut to wide shot of FINISHED WORK. The five-pass refinement collapses into one luminous final frame. Cinematic. Production-grade.**

> *"Same craftsman. Same intent. Sharper blade with every tool in his hands."*

**1:22 – 1:30 — GALLERY MONTAGE. Four to six finished Runway-generated shots in rapid cuts (~1.3s each). Each shot is a different style/subject — proving range. All genuinely produced by the army during the build window.**

> *"What used to take a hundred craftspeople,"*

**1:28 – 1:30 — Hold on the most striking shot.**

> *"now takes one."*

**1:30 – 1:35 — One more deliberate shot. Slow burn. Beautiful.**

> *"The blade does not multiply noise."*

**1:35 – 1:40 — Cut to a close-up of a tool being placed back on the workbench. Intentional. Reverent.**

> *"It multiplies one human's intention."*

---

### ACT 3 — THE THESIS (1:40 – 1:50)

**1:40 – 1:43 — Workbench wide shot. Tools laid out. Light shifting.**

> *"Anyone can be a builder."*

**1:43 – 1:47 — Cut to TODDLER-FLYING photo OR Roger holding his SON at Golden Gate. Real photo. Hold.**

> *"Here's one."*

**1:47 – 1:50 — Beat. Photo holds. Slow audio fade. Workbench dims. Approach the cut.**

---

### CLIMAX — THE CLOSING SHOT (1:50 – 1:58)

**1:50 – 1:52 — AI elements dissolve to black. Total silence for ~0.5 seconds.**

**1:52 – 1:58 — REAL ROGER ON CAMERA** *(Mastermind's recorded clip. Medium close-up. Eyes upper third. Soft front light. Direct address.)*

> *"Cris. We're here for you. Let's talk."*

*[Two beats of silence after "Let's talk." Real Roger holds the camera's eye. Don't cut early.]*

---

### END CARD — 1:58 – 2:02

**1:58 – 2:02 — Fade to brand card.**

> **NUMBER ONE SON SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT**
> Powered by Runway.
> Builders Program — let us talk.

*[End card holds for 4 seconds. Music bed swells one final note. Cut to black.]*

## Voice Recording Script (for Mastermind to record)

This is what Roger reads — clean room, decent mic, slow documentary pace. We extract the lines we need; ElevenLabs will train on the full recording for the cloned voice. Read it through twice in slightly different intonations.

> So I lived. I worked. I built furniture. I built homes. I built commercial buildings. I built businesses. I built a family. And I taught them to fly. And every time the world changed, I asked myself the same question. Am I still living up to it? And then this happened. The smartest AI we've ever experienced. And like every craftsman before me, I picked up the tools. First pass — rough. The shape of an idea. Second pass — clarity. Third pass — the cut. Fourth pass — the voice. Fifth pass — the soul. Same craftsman. Same intent. Sharper blade with every tool in his hands. What used to take a hundred craftspeople, now takes one. The blade does not multiply noise. It multiplies one human's intention. Anyone can be a builder. Here's one.

**Recording instructions:**
- Quiet room, decent mic (wired earbuds or AirPods clipped near collar).
- Read at deliberate pace — documentary cadence, not casual conversation.
- Pause naturally at sentence breaks.
- Read the whole script through twice. Different inflections fine.
- Save as `mastermind_voice_sample.m4a` or `.wav`.
- Upload to Cowork.
