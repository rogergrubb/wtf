## PROMPT 2 — CRITIQUE OUR ACTUAL OPEN SHOT

```
Here's the keystone shot of my brand film. It's the first 5-7 seconds — 
the hook that determines whether judges stay for the next 115.

CONCEPT: Over-the-shoulder cell phone reveal. We open on a close-up of 
a young boy (age 5) playing on a 1969 beach. The shot looks like polished 
modern phone-captured video. As the camera pulls back, we realize we're 
looking at a CELL PHONE SCREEN. Pull-back continues over the shoulder 
of the boy's mother (age 25) holding the phone, framing him. Background: 
other kids splashing in shallow water. Soft shutter click at 5s mark.

WHY IT WORKS: A cell phone in 1969 is impossible. The audience clocks 
the anachronism in 3 seconds. They're now in on the premise — AI 
restoring a moment that almost was — without us narrating a single word. 
The phone IS the metaphor for all the Runway tools used.

I plan to compose this as 4 layers:
- Layer 1 (background): kids splashing in golden hour Russian River water
- Layer 2 (mid): generic-modern phone bezel with screen masked transparent
- Layer 3 (inside phone): young boy on the beach, looks like phone capture
- Layer 4 (foreground): mother's shoulder/arm/hand holding the phone

Final pass: gen4_aleph for unified Kodachrome color grade and film grain.

MASTER STYLE PROMPT (pasted into every gen4.5 prompt):
"Cinematic, intimate, documentary realism. Warm 1969 Kodachrome color 
palette with modern dynamic range — golden saturation, faded shadows, 
soft highlights. Shot on ARRI Alexa with 35mm lens. Subtle film grain. 
Shallow depth of field. Natural light, golden hour. Quiet, observational, 
emotionally honest. Cinematography inspired by Terrence Malick and Roger 
Deakins."

TWO QUESTIONS:

1. What's the single biggest failure risk in this shot from a prompting 
   standpoint? Where will gen4.5 most likely betray us, and what 
   prompt-level insurance do I add now to prevent it?

2. The hardest layer is Layer 4 — the mother back-to-camera holding a 
   2026 phone in 1969. Write me the BEST gen4.5 prompt you can produce

   craft the actual production-grade prompt I would paste into Runway.
```
