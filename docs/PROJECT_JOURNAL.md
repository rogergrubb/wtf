## Tuesday, May 5, 2026 — Day One (the only day so far)

### Strategic decisions made today

1. **Pivoted away from "DirectorOS single-product" to "Number One Son agentic clone-army platform."** The thesis "we built the platform that builds the platform" is stronger than "we built one good product."

2. **Magnum Opus = Origin Documentary, not feature.** The film is itself produced by the system it documents. Recursive structure.

3. **Pivoted from "we'll be Runway's content team" to "we'll be Runway's canonical Builders Program case study."** Reference-customer position, not vendor position. Salesforce-Trailblazer model. Builders Program launched 37 days before the hackathon — slot is empty, we fill it.

4. **Dropped the "clone army / general / soldiers" external vocabulary** in favor of **"craftsman's blade / workbench / passes."** Cris's brand language is craft-coded, not industrial-coded. Star Wars clone troopers are interchangeable; that's slop economy. The blade-sharpening metaphor is genuinely Roger's biography (journeyman carpenter, built furniture/homes/commercial buildings/businesses/family).

5. **Locked the brand film's emotional hook:** *"A son still trying to live up to what his mother saw in him — even now, in the age of artificial intelligence."* Universal. Every viewer with a parent feels it.

6. **Locked the iconic image:** the closing shot. AI dissolves; real Roger looks at camera and says *"Cris, we're here for you. Let's talk."* Seven words. The Mean Joe Greene jersey moment of our film.

7. **Refactored film structure to use real photographs as Act 1 backbone.** Roger's mother bookends Act 1: she's 25 in the AUG 69 beach photo, ~75 in the hospital three-generation photo. Same woman, fifty years apart, contains the entire arc.

8. **Roger's son** — appears in Golden Gate photo. Lineage extends forward: mother → only son → and now Roger's own son. The Number One Son name has a successor.

9. **Submission becomes three artifacts**, each for a different decision-maker:
   - Brand film (~100s) — emotional, mother story, ends on Cris-direct close. *For Cris's gut.*
   - Explainer film (~60-90s) — AI-narrated, exponential growth + anti-slop thesis. *For Cris's CFO.*
   - Clone gallery (46 deployed URLs) — receipts. *For Cris's engineers.*
