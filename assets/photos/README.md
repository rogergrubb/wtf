## The mother bookend

The same woman appears in `1969_mom25_roger5_sister3.png` (age 25) and `hospital_three_generations.png` (age ~75). Fifty years apart. She bookends Act 1 of the brand film. The audience sees her at the start of the arc and at the end of the arc, and the lifetime in between is exactly the lifetime the narration describes.

This is the brand film's emotional load-bearing beam.
